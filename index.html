About Infinity Eye Hospital

Innovation in Sight. Compassion in Care.

Founded with a vision to bring world-class ophthalmic care to Amravati, Infinity Superspeciality Eye Hospital combines state-of-the-art diagnostic technology with a team of highly experienced surgeons dedicated to restoring and preserving your vision.

From routine eye checkups to complex retina surgeries, our 24/7 facility offers the full spectrum of eye care under one roof.

  • 24/7 Emergency Eye Care Availability
  • Advanced AI-Assisted Diagnostic Equipment
  • Team of Highly Experienced Ophthalmologists
  • Fully Sterile, NABH-Compliant Environment
  • Affordable Treatment with Zero Compromise on Quality

Our Expertise

Superspeciality Eye Care

LASIK / Laser Surgery

Experience freedom from glasses with our bladeless LASIK technology. Precision laser correction for myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism. Quick, painless, and permanent results.

Cataract Surgery

Advanced Phacoemulsification (Phaco) and premium IOL implantation for rapid recovery and crystal-clear post-operative vision with minimal downtime.

Retina Treatment

Comprehensive retina care including treatment for diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, retinal detachment, and vitreo-retinal surgeries.

Glaucoma Care

Advanced intraocular pressure monitoring, early glaucoma detection, and surgical or laser interventions to prevent progressive vision loss.

Pediatric Eye Care

Specialized, child-friendly examinations and treatment for lazy eye (amblyopia), squint, refractive errors, and congenital conditions in infants and children.

Dry Eye Treatment

Modern diagnostic and therapeutic protocols for chronic dry eye syndrome, including intense pulsed light therapy, punctal plugs, and medicated eye drops.
